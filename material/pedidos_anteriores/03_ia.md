## AI-native, with our own engine

Casepace was designed around AI from the first line of code. The language
models come from OpenAI [and Anthropic], used through their commercial APIs.
Under those terms, the providers do not use your data to train their models.

Everything around the models is ours.

**The form engine.** Each answer is mapped to its exact field, checkbox and
continuation sheet by rules we wrote from the form instructions. The model
never decides where a value goes.

**The checks.** Dates, names, and the consistency between documents and the
interview are verified before anything reaches the form. If something does
not match, your team sees it.

**Your review.** Nothing is filed without a person on your team reading and
approving it. Casepace prepares; your attorney or accredited representative
decides.
